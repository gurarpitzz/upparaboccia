Uttar Pradesh Para Boccia

State Governing Body for Para Boccia in UP

Officially registered on 18th March 2026 and proudly affiliated with the Boccia Sports Federation of India (BSFI), the Uttar Pradesh Para Boccia Association (UPPBA) unites clinicians, rehabilitation specialists, coaches, and para-athletes across Uttar Pradesh.

Designed for individuals with severe neurological, muscular, and locomotor conditions, Para Boccia combines tactical precision with full accessibility — and UP athletes are already clinching national Gold & Silver medals and World Challenger podiums.

Hospital-to-Court Clinical Model

Spearheaded by the Department of Physical Medicine & Rehabilitation (PMR) at Dr. Ram Manohar Lohia Institute of Medical Sciences (Dr. RMLIMS), Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh pioneered India’s dedicated hospital-based Boccia Rehabilitation Unit for patients recovering from long ICU stays and severe neuromuscular disabilities.

Featured in The Times of India (Jan 2026), UPPBA bridges medical institutions — SGPGI, KGMU, DSMNRU, CRC Lucknow, ALIMCO, and AIIMS Gorakhpur — straight into competitive para-sport.

Inclusive Growth Across All 75 Districts

Our Vision: “To make Boccia accessible to every eligible person with a disability in Uttar Pradesh and empower them to achieve their full potential through sports.”

Our Mission: “To promote inclusive sports, identify talent, facilitate training and create pathways for athletes from Uttar Pradesh to represent the state and the country at national and international platforms.”

Four Paralympic Sport Classes (BC1–BC4)

  • BC1: Athletes with severe cerebral palsy throwing or kicking the ball, permitted an assistant on court.
  • BC2: Cerebral palsy athletes competing independently by hand without assistive ramp support.
  • BC3: Athletes with severe four-limb limitation using an assistive ramp and sports assistant — proudly championed in UP by Ms. Sarita Dwivedi.
  • BC4: Non-cerebral severe physical impairments (muscular dystrophy, spinal cord injury, amputees) — led in UP by 4x National Champion Mr. Jatin Kushwah.
