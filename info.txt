Introduction – Uttar Pradesh Para Boccia Association (UPPBA)

The Uttar Pradesh Para Boccia Association (UPPBA) was officially registered on 18th March 2026 with the primary objective of promoting and developing Boccia as an inclusive para-sport for Persons with Disabilities across Uttar Pradesh.

The Association is affiliated with the Boccia India Federation and is committed to expanding the reach of Boccia at the grassroots level throughout the state.

UPPBA aims to identify and nurture sporting talent among persons with disabilities, provide opportunities for professional training and participation in state, national and international competitions, and create awareness about Boccia as a sport that promotes inclusion, confidence, independence and equal opportunities.

Through collaboration with government institutions, hospitals, rehabilitation centres, educational institutions, sports organisations and other stakeholders, the Association strives to build an accessible and supportive sporting ecosystem.

Our Vision: To make Boccia accessible to every eligible person with a disability in Uttar Pradesh and empower them to achieve their full potential through sports.

Our Mission: To promote inclusive sports, identify talent, facilitate training and create pathways for athletes from Uttar Pradesh to represent the state and the country at national and international platforms.
